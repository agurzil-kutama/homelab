# Home Lab

A working record of my home lab — built through practice, curiosity, and consistency.

This repository documents what I build, configure, break, and rebuild in my own environment. It is not tied to a single platform, tool, vendor, or discipline. It grows with me as my focus shifts and my skills expand.

---

## Purpose

Turn practice into evidence.

Certifications prove I studied. This repo proves I built, tested, broke, and understood. It is a space for experiments, configurations, notes, and lessons learned from hands-on work.

---

## Philosophy

- Learn by building, not just reading.
- Document the process, not only the result.
- Break things on purpose, then fix them.
- Understand the "why" behind every decision.
- Stay curious, stay honest, stay consistent.
- Build in silence, let the work speak.

---

## How This Repo Is Organized

Over time, this repo will evolve. Folders, categories, and formats may change as my focus shifts and my skills grow. What stays constant is the intent: clean, honest, useful documentation of real work.

---

## Who This Is For

- **Me** — as a reference, a revision tool, and a record of progress.
- **Recruiters and hiring managers** — as proof of practical skill and consistency.
- **Anyone building their own lab** — as notes that might help them on their path.
